INT. APARTMENT KITCHEN - NIGHT

Rain taps against the windows. MAYA places two untouched cups of coffee on the table.

JONAH
You kept the blue one.

MAYA
It was a cup, Jonah. Not a promise.

JONAH
Everything was a promise with you.

MAYA
No. Everything was evidence with you.

Jonah looks toward the hallway. A moving box blocks the doorway.

JONAH
Are you leaving tonight?

MAYA
The train is at eleven.

JONAH
You hate night trains.

MAYA
I hate goodbyes more.

JONAH
Then don't make this one.

MAYA
You made it six months ago. I'm just finally saying the line.

Jonah reaches for the coffee but stops.

JONAH
I came back.

MAYA
You came back because the other door closed.

JONAH
That's not fair.

MAYA
Neither was waiting.

A long silence. The rain gets heavier.

JONAH
Tell me what to do.

MAYA
Listen.

JONAH
I'm listening.

MAYA
No. You're waiting for your turn to fix it.

JONAH
Maybe I can.

MAYA
Then tell me why you left.

JONAH
I was afraid that staying meant I had chosen one life forever.

MAYA
And now?

JONAH
Now I know leaving was still a choice.

Maya picks up the blue cup and hands it to him.

MAYA
Drink your coffee before it gets cold.

JONAH
Is that all?

MAYA
For tonight.

JONAH
And tomorrow?

MAYA
Tomorrow you can ask a better question.

Jonah takes the cup. Maya moves the box out of the doorway.

FADE OUT.
